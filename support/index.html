Harbourline service desk

Every fix here comes word for word from the IT team's guides.

Pick your problem. iMBrace files a ticket, its service-desk assistant picks the fix guide for your product and version, and you get that guide's steps, word for word.

How it works

  1. Choose your problem below.
  2. GitHub opens the request form with it filled in. Press Create.
  3. iMBrace files a ticket, and its service-desk assistant picks the fix guide for that product and version.
  4. iMBrace checks the guide fits, then replies on your request with the guide's steps.

Ask the service desk

Send the request on GitHub

You need a GitHub account, and requests are public. Choose from the list only.

  • The two SecureLink VPN versions get different fixes for the same error.
  • Choose "version not known" to see the assistant ask for the version instead of guessing.
  • No guide covers PrintHub yet: choose it to see the assistant say so instead of guessing a fix.

The fix guides

The IT team's own guides, kept in iMBrace's knowledge library. Each one applies to one product and version.

    Harbourline Insurance is fictional. Tickets are filed on an iMBrace DataIQ board; an iMBrace agent reads them against the fix guides in its knowledge library; FlowOps checks its choice and posts the guide's own words.
