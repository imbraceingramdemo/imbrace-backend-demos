iMBrace as a website's backend

Websites that run on iMBrace

Each site below is a plain web page. iMBrace keeps its records, runs its checks and sends its replies. Visitors choose from fixed options, and iMBrace does the work every time.

The sites

  • Live demo

    Orvik catalogue

    Request a product description. iMBrace drafts it from the product's facts and checks every figure, a person approves it, and it is published on the page.

  • Live demo

    Harbourline service desk

    Choose a problem. iMBrace files a ticket, its assistant picks the fix guide for that product and version, and you get the guide's own steps.

Orvik and the other companies on these sites are fictional. Requests are made on GitHub and are public.
