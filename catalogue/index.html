How it works

  1. Choose a product and a note below.
  2. GitHub opens the request form with your choices. Press Create.
  3. iMBrace files the request, drafts the description from the product's facts, and checks every figure.
  4. A person approves it. iMBrace publishes it here and replies on your request.
